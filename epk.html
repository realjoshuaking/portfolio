Short bio

Joshua King is a self-taught music producer and composer with 8+ years of experience across pop, orchestral, hip-hop, R&B, electronic, and cinematic music. He has collaborated with Markiplier and Schmoyoho on "The Barrel" (8M+ streams/views), produced the lo-fi EP Blue Flowers, studio albums with vocal group ALIVE, and his 2026 debut orchestral album Legato.

Every note he delivers is human-composed and produced — he does not use AI generation in his musical work.

Long bio

Joshua King is a self-taught music producer and composer whose work moves fluidly between pop, orchestral, hip-hop, R&B, jazz, rock, electronic, ambient, cinematic, latin, lo-fi, and chill. Over eight-plus years, he has built a catalog that spans comedy-pop records with internet culture's biggest creators, intimate lo-fi EPs, full vocal-group albums, and large-scale orchestral writing.

His credits include the Markiplier × Schmoyoho collaboration "The Barrel" (8M+ streams/views), the lo-fi EP Blue Flowers, studio albums with his sisters' vocal group ALIVE, and his debut orchestral album Legato (2026). He works from a toolkit anchored by FL Studio, NI Komplete 15 Ultimate, BBC Symphony Orchestra Pro, and Arturia vintage synths.

Joshua composes and produces for artists, indie creators, games, and visual media — and all of his music is human-composed and produced, with no AI generation in his creative work.
